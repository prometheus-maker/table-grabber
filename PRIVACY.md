# Privacy

Table Grabber collects nothing.

- It runs only when you click its button (or press Alt+Shift+G), and only in that tab.
- It reads the page you are looking at to find tables and lists. When you press "Get pages", it loads the site's
  next pages the same way your browser would, from that same site, with a short pause between pages.
- Files are made inside your browser and saved by your browser. Nothing is uploaded, and there is no server.
- No analytics, no tracking, no account, no ads.

Permissions: `activeTab` (the tab you clicked it in) and `scripting` (to open the panel in that tab). That's all.
